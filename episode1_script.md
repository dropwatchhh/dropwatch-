# Episode 1: "Live and Rolling" (script v2, for your approval; nothing has been made)

**Series:** Dex Hollow's 30 Days to Showtime. **Length: 66 s** (never under 62 s). **Vote:** CHASE or PLAY.
**Premise:** Dex is livestreaming from a parking lot, bragging he can play through anything. Behind him, his van, with his only guitar inside, rolls away with Gig at the wheel. Lena joins the stream. The van is heading for Nana Dot's flower stand.
**Stakes (clear by 0:05):** Lena's pinned message on the stream says **"NO GUITAR = NO HEADLINE SHOW."** If Dex loses his guitar, he loses the biggest show of his life.
**Catchphrase:** "It's fine. It's rock 'n' roll!" (0:14 and 0:55). **Vote prompt:** "Your call, crew."
**Rules I'm following:** open on the action, a new surprise at least every 5 seconds, no shot over 4 seconds, big 1–3 word captions, and a vote card for the last 8 seconds.

## Cast in this episode
Dex (neon punk). **Gig** the raccoon (driver, no words, only chitters and on-screen text). **Lena Cho** the promoter (a video tile on the stream, calm voice). **Nana Dot** (flower stand, sunglasses on a chain, bowling jacket). **Priya** (noodle cart, always has his order ready).
