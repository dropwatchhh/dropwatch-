**No other voices.** No Lena, Nana Dot or GPS voice is created. Gig has only chitters, squeaks and the horn (sound effects, not a voice).

## How the reference videos handle voices (see the findings I sent with this)
One continuous narrator tells everything in short deadpan sentences. Other characters' words are **reported by the narrator** ("Grandma said, 'Again.'", "He said, 'Just water.'"), not spoken in their own voices. I could not hear whether the characters' mouths move; see the limits in the findings.

## The same idea in our story
**Dex is the one voice, and the story is his livestream.** He is live on camera talking to chat the whole time, so one voice telling everything is natural: he reports what Lena and Nana Dot say to chat ("Lena just joined. Why's her headliner's guitar driving away?"), reads chat out loud, and yells in the moment. The story, the stakes and the ending stay the same.

## Lip-sync rules (my reading of the references; tell me if you want different)
- **Dex's mouth moves with the voice only when he is on screen and the line is him talking** (the brag, yelling at Gig, "Oh. Oh no. Oh YES.", the catchphrase).
- **When the voice reports what someone else said** (Lena, Nana Dot), that person is shown reacting **with a closed or set mouth, never mouthing the words**. The words also pop up as on-screen captions.
- **When Dex is off screen** (cab, call cards, street, stand), nobody mouths the voice-over. Characters show expressions and body action only.
